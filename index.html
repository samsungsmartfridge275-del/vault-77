READ BEFORE YOU START

About This Platform

The Tell is a just-for-you puzzle range, not a live contest against other players — your score, progress, and activity log only track your own solving, saved right in your own browser. Nothing here is sent anywhere, and there's no real "other team" racing you.

How Flags Are Checked

Every flag you submit is checked right here on your own device using a scrambling trick called a hash — the answers themselves are never hidden anywhere in the page's code, so peeking at the code won't give you a shortcut. You really do have to solve each puzzle!

General Rules

  1. Flags always look like flag{...} and you can type them in any mix of capital or lowercase letters.
  2. Once you solve a challenge, it's solved for good — you'll see it checked off right away.
  3. You can use anything that helps you think — paper, a calculator, a friend, or even your browser's "View Page Source" — that's part of the fun!
  4. Every challenge is worth a fixed number of points, no matter how long it takes you.
  5. There are no extra hints beyond what's written on each card, but the difficulty tag tells you roughly what to expect.
